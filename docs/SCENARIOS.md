# SIH26037 — Benchmark Scenarios (5 Hallmark Indian Road Challenges)

### 1. Unmarked Narrow Village Road with Lateral Drop-offs
- Narrow 4.5m paved road, no lane markers, open drainage ditches on shoulders.
- Oncoming slow tractor; ego vehicle must nudge laterally while maintaining safe distance from ditch.

### 2. Unsignalled Chaotic Urban Multi-Way Intersection
- Wide 4-way intersection without traffic lights.
- Auto-rickshaws crossing perpendicular, motorcycles filtering through gaps.
- Ego vehicle negotiates right-of-way via time-to-arrival and courtesy yielding.

### 3. Aggressive Three-Wheeler Cut-In & Highway Merge
- Auto-rickshaw on shoulder aggressively swerves in front of ego vehicle (TTC < 1.4s).
- Ego activates controlled braking or evasive nudge to avoid collision.

### 4. High-Density Pedestrian & Pushcart Market Street
- Tight 5.0m corridor with fruit/vegetable pushcart parked on side and pedestrians crossing.
- Low-speed creeping (v <= 10 km/h) with predictive collision avoidance.

### 5. Stationary Stray Cattle Roadblock on Blind Curvature
- Cows resting in the middle of a blind curve.
- Sensor detects roadblock within 25m; emergency stop engages, followed by slow skirt-around.
